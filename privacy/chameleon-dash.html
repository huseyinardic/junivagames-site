Privacy Policy for Chameleon Dash

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Juniva Games ("we", "us") handles information when you play Chameleon Dash ("the App").

Who We Are

Chameleon Dash is developed by Juniva Games (https://junivagames.com). You can contact us at junivagames@gmail.com.

Information Stored on Your Device

The App does not require an account. Your game progress (best score, daily streak, unlocked color themes, and settings such as sound, music, and vibration) is stored only on your device and is not sent to us.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how players use the App so we can improve it (for example, how long games last, whether players complete the tutorial, and how often features are used). Firebase may collect:

  • App usage events, such as game sessions, scores, and use of in-game features
  • Device information, such as device model, operating system version, and app version
  • An app instance identifier and, where permitted, the advertising identifier
  • Approximate location (country or region) derived from your IP address
  • Advertising performance data from Google AdMob, such as ad impressions and the revenue they generate

We do not use analytics data to identify you personally. Where consent is required by law (such as in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland), the App does not collect analytics data.

Learn more about how Firebase handles data: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

Advertising

The App shows ads served by Google AdMob, including optional rewarded ads (for example, to continue a run or unlock a color theme). AdMob may collect and use data such as advertising identifiers, device information, and IP address to show ads, measure their performance, and prevent fraud, and may show personalized ads where permitted. This may include the use of cookies or similar technologies.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, we ask for your consent through Google's consent form before your data is used for personalized ads. To change your choices, clear the App's data (Settings → Apps → Chameleon Dash → Storage → Clear data) and the consent form will be shown again the next time you open the App, or contact us.

You can learn more about how Google uses this data here: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (for example, Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Data collected by Google AdMob and Google Analytics for Firebase is processed by Google in accordance with its privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Data Retention

Analytics data is kept in Google Analytics for a limited period according to our data retention settings and is then deleted automatically. Game progress stays on your device until you uninstall the App or clear its data.

Your Choices and Rights

You can reset or delete your advertising ID and, where applicable, change your consent choices as described above. Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR or Turkey's KVKK), you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your data. To exercise these rights, contact us at junivagames@gmail.com.

Children's Privacy

This App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact us at: junivagames@gmail.com
