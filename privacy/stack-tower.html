Privacy Policy for Stack Tower

Last updated: August 2026

Stack Tower itself does not require account creation and does not collect personal information directly. Your game progress (high score) is stored only on your device.

This App displays ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use data (such as advertising identifiers and general device information) to show ads, including personalized ads, in accordance with Google's own privacy policy. This may include the use of cookies or similar technologies.

You can learn more about how Google uses this data here: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

You can manage your ad personalization settings through your device's settings (e.g., Google Settings → Ads on Android).

Children's Privacy

This App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data Sharing

We do not sell or share any personally identifiable information. Any data collected by third-party advertising services (AdMob) is governed by their respective privacy policies.
